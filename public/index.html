Questions

Can my clients download the photos?

No. They only see small watermarked previews. You print from your full-quality originals.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open the link, pick their photos and send. That is all.

Can clients change their mind?

Yes, until they press Send. After that you can reopen the gallery for them in one click.

What happens if I cancel?

Your clients can no longer open their galleries and you cannot upload. You can still log in and download your originals.

Can someone take a screenshot?

No website can fully stop screenshots. The watermark and small preview size make a screenshot unsuitable for printing.
